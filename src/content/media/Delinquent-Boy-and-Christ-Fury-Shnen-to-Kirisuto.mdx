### The Book That Shaped Who I Am Today

\
Ango Sakaguchi (1906–1955) was a Japanese writer active in the chaos of postwar Japan. Alongside Osamu Dazai and others, he was labeled a member of the Buraiha("Decadent School"), and he relentlessly attacked conventional morality and hypocrisy. He argued that only by falling all the way into "degeneration" can a person reach their true humanity and a truthful way of living, and he came to exert immense influence as a writer who embodied his era.

Ango left behind a vast number of essays, and nearly all of them are marked by an extraordinary sharpness: always fierce, always sorrowful, always tender, and always beautiful.

He is one of the writers who shaped me most in my teens, and I'd say thirty percent of my personality was formed by Ango Sakaguchi.

Out of that enormous body of essays, the one I deliberately chose is "Delinquent Boy and Christ."

It was written when Osamu Dazai took his own life, yet it is not the kind of piece you could call a eulogy. While taking the form of a merciless attack on Dazai, it is at the same time a merciless defense of him. It builds an acrobatic style out of that contradiction, and that style is both sorrowful and beautiful.

You'll understand as soon as you read it: Ango wrote this dead drunk.

### Japanese

\
坂口安吾（1906–1955）は、戦後の混乱期に活躍した日本の文学者で、太宰治らとともに「無頼派」と称され、既存の道徳や偽善を鋭く批判し続けた。

人間は「堕落」しきることで初めて本来の人間らしさや真実の生き方に到達できると説き、時代を象徴する文学者として多大なる影響を与えることとなった.\
安吾は膨大な数のエッセイを残しており、そのほとんどが、卓越した鋭さに満ち、常に激しく、常に悲しく、常に優しく、そして常に美しい。

\
僕が10代の頃に最も影響を受けた文学者の一人であり、僕の人格の30%は坂口安吾で形成されている。

その膨大なるエッセイ群の中で、あえて選んだのが、この『不良少年とキリスト』だ。

\
これは太宰治が自殺したときに書かれたエッセイだが、追悼文と呼べる種類のものではない。\
徹底的に太宰治を批判するという体裁をとりながら、徹底的に太宰治を擁護しているというアクロバティックな文体を構築しており、その文体は悲しくも美しい。

一読すればわかるだろうが、坂口安吾は泥酔しながらこれを書いている。
