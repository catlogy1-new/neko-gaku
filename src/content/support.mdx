introduction: |-
  Because addiction doesn’t happen in a vacuum. If you are facing a mental health crisis, violence, abuse, or discrimination, please reach out to these support networks.

  At the root of all addictions (including self-harm) lie underlying issues such as a history of mental illness, abuse, bullying, all forms of discrimination, domestic violence, and growing up in a dysfunctional family.
  Since many people struggling with addiction are unaware of this, I want to convey this message through this publication.

  I’ve included a list of counseling services for all kinds of problems, not just substance abuse.
  These are limited to the U.S. and Japan, however.
addictionNote: In reality, these are useless to those currently in active addiction.
safetyIntroduction: |-
  This is important.

  It’s an issue that cannot be ignored when considering addiction.
afterword: |-
  To be honest, most active addicts already know where to find help. They stay trapped in addiction despite knowing these resources, so this list might seem meaningless.
  However, addiction is often rooted in underlying mental health issues or unresolved trauma.

  My hope is that this Publication can serve as a catalyst for recognizing what lies beneath.
  Please make use of these resources.
