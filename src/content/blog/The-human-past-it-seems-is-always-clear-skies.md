## The human past, it seems, is always clear skies

Ango Sakaguchi used to say this all the time. When Ango died, Jun Ishikawa opened his memorial essay by quoting it.\
Ango faced life and death in earnest, to a degree you could never compare with Osamu Dazai and the like. He agonized, grieved, and was wounded, and in Yukio Mishima's words, "fought it out splendidly to the end." And that very Ango, in the end, spat out a line like this.

So very Ango.

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

From the age of three to seven, I grew up in a home of domestic violence and abuse.\
My father was a yakuza, an alcoholic, a violent drunk, and almost every day we ran from him.

When we heard him coming home, raging, my mother, my sister, and I, the three of us, would flee the house no matter how deep in the night, and simply keep walking until morning.

Once, in the middle of winter, we ran out with no time to put on shoes, and the three of us walked barefoot until dawn.\
I remember a man passing by, turning to look at us — yes, I am playing with that scene right now, in the present tense.

That day, the three of us attempted a small trespass.\
We climbed over the fence at the entrance to a construction site, an expressway under construction in our neighborhood, and walked out to the unfinished edge.

One more step and we would have fallen to our deaths, a precarious place, but the view from there, the streets and the city, was so beautiful.\
Above all, there it was: a landscape that no one in the world knew, ours alone.

It wasn't painful at all.\
The three of us laughed.\
I (three) and my sister (six) don't remember this, but apparently my mother said to us then, "Shall we die?"\
Apparently we answered at once: "No."

To the world we must have looked like a miserable family, one nobody wanted to touch, but we were enchanted by the view in front of us.\
Death never crossed our minds, not even a little.\
It was a landscape only we could see, a treasure only we owned… really?\
Maybe I only vaguely feel that it was so — yes. That is to say, this part is in the past tense.

But the three of us laughing, that much is certain.\
Whether it was real or not, it is certain.

\
"The human past, it seems, is always clear skies."
