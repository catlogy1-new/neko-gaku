## Right? This is just a bunch of nonsense.

I am a former drug addict.

I have used all kinds of illegal drugs in the past, but the one I used the longest was meth. It has been 7years since I quit meth. Now, I have absolutely no craving for drugs.

To you who are suffering from drug addiction right now: Regardless of whether the drugs you are using are illegal or legal, please set aside judgments of right and wrong based on legal perspectives or social norms for the moment.

Those are not the fundamental issues.

Right now, in this moment, you are using drugs to survive.

That’s exactly why I would never say something like, “You should quit drugs right now,” nor would I refer you to a rehab facility for drug addiction.

A referral?

You already know about that without needing a referral, right?

For you right now, those approaches would be completely meaningless.

Because in this very moment, however clumsily, you are desperately trying to live.

Taking drugs away from you in this moment is like taking away the crutches you need to walk.

We drug addicts are clumsy people who can't live well.

There's no need to force yourself to quit drugs. For you right now, they're simply indispensable.

Maybe someday you won't need drugs anymore, or maybe you'll need them until you die.

I'll say it again: "You don't have to quit drugs right now."

But one thing I can say for sure is that a life without drugs isn't so bad, you know?

If you're trying to quit drugs right now, just stop for today. If you can, tell yourself to do the same tomorrow.

To those suffering from drug addiction now, to those who just can't quit, to those desperately trying to quit—whatever your situation, may tomorrow be a wonderful day for you.
