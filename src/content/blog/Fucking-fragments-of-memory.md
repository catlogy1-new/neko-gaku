## That's right. It's the kind of rubbish you wanted

A newborn baby is a special being, one for which I can find no words.\
A baby's eyes have not a speck of cloudiness. Like Medusa's gaze, they seem to see through all my stray thoughts and ugliness, leaving me flustered, my thoughts torn right out of my head.\
\
When this child was killed, the doctor told me, the moment I brought the baby in, that there was "not even a 1% chance of survival."\
The brain had swollen and was pressing against the skull, so the skull had been opened and the brain left exposed. The baby was in the ICU on life support, but the brain was already dead, and all that remained was to wait for the heart and lungs to stop.\
\
Inside the ICU, the baby who had been laughing only yesterday lay in front of me in a vegetative state, rushing toward death.\
A scene like that can't be interpreted in language. My brain couldn't register it, and I just stared blankly at the apparition. It was an apparition.\
\
The moment I stepped out of the ICU, I couldn't even walk. I ended up on my knees, face to the floor like a man begging forgiveness, and collapsed in tears. In an instant, the undeniable reality of death hit me.\
\
Human tears have no limit.\
They pour out endlessly and never stop.\
\
When I went back into the ICU, all emotion vanished, and again I was staring at that hazy apparition. Then I'd step out and break down again. It was the same thing, over and over.\
I only learned this during the trial: the hospital nurses had told the police that my behavior in the ICU was "suspicious." They meant that "the one who killed the child might be that guy."

You've probably seen it in movies, TV dramas, novels, in those worlds of entertainment.\
\
The scene where someone grips the hand of a person in a vegetative state, a person 100% headed for death, and cries out, "Hang in there!" "I believe in you!"\
\
Have any of you ever had your child killed?\
This isn't a TV drama.

Fuck all of you.
