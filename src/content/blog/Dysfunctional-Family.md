## Huh? Oh, right, this is just another piece of rubbish, too.

I was seven the first time I stabbed someone with a knife.

I grew up in a home with extreme domestic violence. Almost every day, my drunk father would beat my mother until she collapsed, covered in blood, and had to be taken away in an ambulance. That started from when I was old enough to remember anything, so around two or three.

I can't remember ever sleeping peacefully at night. We slept in our clothes and shoes so we could run at any moment. When my father started raging, we'd head straight outside, and my mother, my sister and I would just walk until morning.

When knives came out, my mom would say, "Go call the police." So my six-year-old sister and I, three years old, would walk to the police station in the middle of the night. But back then the police didn't get involved in "domestic matters." Even with two little kids begging for help in the dead of night, the officer just laughed and said, "Come back after you've been stabbed. Can't do anything till then."

Because of our family, the neighbors hated us. And I had a developmental disorder and couldn't do what everyone else did, so I was bullied relentlessly. My sister was an outcast too, but she was strong at the core, and whenever she saw me getting bullied, she'd throw herself into protecting me.

That sister broke down crying at the police station. A six-year-old and a three-year-old, abandoned by every adult in the world and told to hurry back home to that brutal, violent house. Of course she cried. But she wasn't the type to cry over something like that. I guess she'd finally reached her limit.

Seeing my sister cry, I, who was a crybaby, refused to cry no matter what. I swore I'd take revenge on the whole world, including the cop grinning in front of me. At just three years old.

The first time I was picked up (well, not exactly "picked up") was in nursery school, when I got caught shoplifting four or five times. The next was in first grade, when I broke into a neighbor's house and smashed all their appliances. I don't know why I did it. I've done break-ins more than five times without getting caught. Every time, I didn't steal anything. I just destroyed things.

The next time I was caught was also in first grade. I took a knife I'd stolen from a nearby supermarket and stabbed one of the kids who bullied me in the stomach, as hard as I could. The reason was simple: I wanted to kill him.

The next time was in second grade, less than three months after the first stabbing. This time I stole a longer knife and stabbed another kid who'd been bullying me, again in the stomach, as hard as I could. Of course, I meant to kill him too.

By then the local government, the police, and the school were all trying to work out how to deal with "this monster" (meaning me). But I kept committing crimes at a steady pace and kept getting taken into custody and arrested.

Juvenile classification homes normally only take kids from age 12, but I was committed at ten as an exception, as a dangerous "child offender under the age of criminal responsibility."

But I never felt remorse, and I never would. It was all just fun. Of course it was. I had the time of my life.

Sorry for making you uncomfortable. But I've lived a life several times more uncomfortable than yours, so that's where this story ends.
